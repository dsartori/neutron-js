Neutron - Rules

Neutron is a two-player strategy game invented by Robert A. Kraus.

The objective of the game is to either move the neutron to your home row or to block the neutron so that your opponent cannot move it.

Each player controls the pieces of their colour, and the neutron.
Click a piece to select it, then choose a target square (highlighted) to slide it.
Pieces slide in straight lines until they reach an obstacle.
On turn 1, White moves a piece. After that, move the neutron, then a piece.
Move the neutron to your home row or block the neutron's movement to win.
